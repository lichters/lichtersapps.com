How I work

See first, then decide

You get a draft of your app in the new design before you pay anything. The starting point is the App Store reviews of your app.

Look and ease of use

This is about the look and the ease of use. If you wish, I will build the design into your app myself after delivery, at no extra cost. We agree on scope and date in writing first. If your team prefers to build it, it gets all files and my support, also at no extra cost. Where a screen needs more than design, such as data from your servers, the list of screens says so before you approve it; otherwise your data and workflows stay as they are.

Everything for your developers

Screens for iPhone and Android as image files, design rules as a PDF, transfer of usage rights. For the iPhone, also a SwiftUI project that runs clickable in the Xcode simulator.

Fixed price, in writing, fast

Quote and coordination by email. Before your order, I send you the list of screens, and the fixed price applies to exactly the list you approve. Delivery within 10 working days of the contract and your approval of the list of screens. You pay nothing before March 2027, so it stays out of this year's budget: no advance payment, and the full amount is due in March 2027, after delivery and your approval.

About me

I work on app design and development in Krefeld, Germany. My work has a clear focus: I redesign the iPhone and Android apps that people rely on at work, so that they feel calm, clear and fast.

I read what the users of your app write in their App Store reviews, take their frustration seriously and turn it into a concrete new design. You see the result before you decide anything.

Why I do this work

I am David Lichters, and here in Krefeld I design, build and publish my own iPhone apps.

I believe that software used every working day deserves the same care as the best consumer apps. That shapes my values: respect for the people who use the app, clarity instead of decoration, and honesty toward the companies I work for.

What I specialize in

My design is calm and familiar, in the spirit of Apple's own apps: large, legible type, clear buttons, generous spacing and nothing that competes for attention.

I change the look and the ease of use. For the design I need no access to your systems. If you want me to implement it, we agree on scope and date in writing first.

My service promise

Working with me is simple and predictable. Everything happens in writing by email, so every decision is documented and nothing gets lost in a phone call. My prices are fixed prices, agreed before the work starts: no surprises from hourly billing and no open-ended invoices. From the first message to the handover, you have one contact person who knows your app, your users and your goals.

A delivery you can plan around

My work is delivered digitally, and fast. Within 10 working days of the contract and your approval of the list of screens, you receive the complete handover of the design by email:

  • every screen, designed for iPhone and Android
  • clear design rules your team can follow as the app grows

Because everything arrives in one organized package, your developers can start right away, whether your team is in Germany or elsewhere.

Write to me about your app

If your company's app is part of people's working day, I am glad to help make that day a little easier. Write to me and tell me about your product. You get a clear, written answer from me, and I look after your project from start to finish.
